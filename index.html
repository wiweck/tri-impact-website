What we are

People. Planet. Prosperity. Each depends on the other.

Tri-Impact began with a decade of work focused on workers and their children in textile production. In 2022, we expanded our mission to include nature-responsive production and climate action.

Worker well-being depends on viable livelihoods. Livelihoods depend on healthy ecosystems. Climate resilience depends on communities, institutions and businesses acting together. That interdependence is the meaning of Tri-Impact Global, and the reason its vision reads people, planet and prosperity together.

Our method

The Neev Cycle: built on what stands.

The Neev Cycle begins from what a partner already does well, builds change they continue to own, and documents every step. Four principles hold it together: Appreciative Approach, Trust-Based Engagement, Co-Creation and Measurable Progress.

The method builds on SEED-SCALE.

The Neev Cycle

A seven-step cycle built on four principles: Appreciative Approach, Trust-Based Engagement, Co-Creation and Measurable Progress.

Four principles Appreciative Approach Trust-Based Engagement Co-Creation Measurable Progress
  1. Identify willing partners
  2. Map strengths and successes
  3. Build trust through success dialogues
  4. Capture stakeholder expectations
  5. Education and feedback
  6. Map the full ecosystem
  7. Implement, document and iterate
Initiatives in Focus

Work that leaves sustainable change behind.

Tri-Impact's initiatives are designed to be transferable. Each begins in the field, generates documented evidence at scale, and concludes with a method, tool and pathway that partners and communities can carry forward independently. Success is measured not by activity completed, but by capacity built and sustained.

Climate Action + Livelihood Security

An ecological problem becomes a material opportunity.

Natural Colours documents dye-yielding native and invasive plants, converts field knowledge into practical learning resources and creates a foundation for climate-responsive production and livelihood development.

  • 01 Bilingual handbook for field and artisan use
  • 02 GPS-tagged catalogue of dye-yielding plants
  • 03 Three practical learning modules
