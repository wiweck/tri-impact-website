Legal and Compliance

Tri-Impact Global is a public charitable trust registered in India, formerly known as GoodWeave India Trust. In 2022, the Trust adopted its current name to reflect the full scope of its work. The name change carried no alteration to the Trust's objectives, statutory registrations, approvals, licences or banking arrangements.

Tax and donor eligibility

Eligible for tax-exempt status and 80G donations.

Tri-Impact Global holds 12A and 80G registrations under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Indian donors making eligible contributions may claim tax deductions in accordance with applicable provisions and their own tax position.

FCRA and CSR status

Registered to receive foreign contributions and implement CSR activities.

Tri-Impact Global holds registration under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010, authorising it to receive international contributions for charitable purposes. The organisation is also registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs as an approved CSR implementing organisation, enabling companies to direct qualifying expenditure through Tri-Impact's programmes.
