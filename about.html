OUR STORY

Founded on a field observation. Shaped by what followed.

Tri-Impact Global commenced operations in Varanasi in October 2012, initially under the name GoodWeave India Trust. Its objective was to build field systems that would protect workers and children at the base of textile production.

The organisation worked inside handmade carpet, apparel and textile communities where people producing for national and international markets remained outside the formal systems meant to reach them. Health care, social protection, education and responsible employment were all designed on paper to cover these workers. In practice, few of the institutions responsible had the local relationships or operational reach to deliver.

Years of field work showed that these conditions could not be addressed separately. A worker's health affected household income, income affected whether children stayed in school, and market pressure on wages weighed on the survival of heritage skills as much as climate stress weighed on migration and economic insecurity.

On 5 July 2022, the Trust adopted the name Tri-Impact Global. The change reflected a wider mandate, shaped by a changing operating environment and by needs the original remit no longer covered. The name recognises three goals that had become inseparable in practice: People, Planet and Prosperity.

Today, Tri-Impact applies the field knowledge, relationships and institutional experience built since 2012 to programmes that connect human well-being, ecological resilience and viable livelihoods.

WHO WE ARE

Three focus areas. One connected system.

Environmental pressure, insecure work and fragile livelihoods converge on the same households, in the parts of the economy where Tri-Impact works.

People, Planet and Prosperity express the organisation's vision. They are not programmes. They guide three connected focus areas.

01

Climate Action

Across climate mitigation, adaptation and resilience, Tri-Impact supports nature-responsive livelihoods, strengthens community and institutional responses to environmental change, and generates knowledge that can inform policy, finance and scalable solutions.

02

Decent Work

Health care, financial access, social protection and responsible employment systems move closer to workers in labour-intensive production through this focus area. Tri-Impact also builds the capacity of businesses to understand their own production networks and act on what they find.

03

Livelihood Security

Heritage skills, local enterprise and environmentally responsible production depend on economic conditions that this focus area works to strengthen, so that valuable knowledge and work remain viable.

These focus areas do not operate in isolation. A single programme or initiative may contribute to more than one.

ONE METHOD ACROSS IMPLEMENTATION WORK IN EACH FOCUS AREA

A consistent method. Adapted to each setting.

Within the on-the-ground implementation work across the three focus areas, the Neev Cycle provides a shared discipline for building trust, understanding what already works, capturing stakeholder expectations, mapping the wider system and documenting change.

The method remains consistent. Its application changes with the people, institutions and conditions involved.

GOVERNANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Clear responsibilities. Verifiable compliance.

Tri-Impact Global is governed by a Board of Trustees under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882.

The Board holds responsibility for fiduciary oversight, institutional direction and the review of major financial and programme commitments. The CEO and executive team are responsible for implementation, management and reporting.

The organisation's financial accounts are audited annually by an independent chartered accountant.

Governance documents available on written request include the Trust Deed, audited financial statements, FCRA annual returns and CSR-1 registration documentation.

Requests for governance information may be sent to contact@tri-impact.org.
